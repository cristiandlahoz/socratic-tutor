# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and educators. Students use the tutor to reason through algorithms, documents, chats, and evaluations; educators need the product to feel trustworthy enough for academic learning workflows.

## Product Purpose

Tutor Socratico helps learners explore ideas, resolve doubts, practice algorithmic thinking, and receive guided Socratic support through chat, document ingestion, and evaluations. Success means users can focus on learning and reasoning without UI friction.

## Brand Personality

Editorial, rigorous, calm, academic, crafted.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, interchangeable AI-app gradients, sterile blue productivity UI, and game-like achievement-heavy interfaces.

## Design Principles

- Learning first: visual polish should clarify the task, not compete with it.
- Academic confidence: typography and spacing should feel deliberate, mature, and trustworthy.
- Guided focus: primary flows should reduce cognitive noise and keep attention on reasoning.
- Consistent craft: forms, navigation, states, and feedback should share one component vocabulary.
- Expressive restraint: the crow and editorial styling are signature moments, not decoration everywhere.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Maintain strong contrast, visible keyboard focus, accessible error messaging, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion alternatives for non-essential animation.
